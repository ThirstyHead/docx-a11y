About docx-a11y

The Social Model of Disability

docx-a11y is built on the Social Model of Disability. This model posits that people are disabled by barriers in society (such as inaccessible documents), not by their impairments. By removing these barriers, we create a world where everyone can access information regardless of how they interact with it.

WCAG 2.1 Coverage

Our auditing engine targets the following WCAG 2.1 success criteria:

Criterion Requirement Check
1.1.1 Non-text Content Alt-text presence and quality
1.3.1 Info and Relationships Heading hierarchy and table headers
2.4.3 Focus Order Logical reading order of elements
4.1.2 Name, Role, Value Correct semantic tagging of document parts
