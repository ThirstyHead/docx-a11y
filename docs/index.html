Make your Word documents accessible to everyone.

docx-a11y is a powerful, open-source tool that audits .docx files for WCAG compliance, helping you identify and fix accessibility barriers in seconds.

Why docx-a11y?

Fast Auditing

Scan entire folders of documents instantly. No more manual checking of every heading and alt-text field.

Interactive Triage

Our GUI allows you to review issues and mark them as fixed or ignored in a streamlined workflow.

Agentic AI Ready

Built with a CLI and API that allow LLMs to audit and suggest fixes for your documents automatically.
